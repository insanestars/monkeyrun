How to play

Coco the capuchin just swiped the Sun Banana from the Temple of Kahu-Ra. The jaguar Guardian is awake. Run!

←→
Turn & dodge
At a corner, ← / → turns. Everywhere else it changes lanes. Swipe on touch.
↑Space
Jump
Over logs, roots, spike walls, rubble and gaps.
↓
Slide
Under stone arches, branches and fire jets. In the air it slams you down.
Esc
Pause
Also pauses when you switch tabs.
Dangers
  • Roots trip you up. Trip twice while the Guardian is close and you're caught.
  • Logs, spike walls, pillars, statues, boulders stop you dead.
  • Gaps & narrow bridges drop into the misty chasm.
  • Dead ends: miss a turn and you hit the temple wall.
Power-ups
  • 🧲 Banana Magnet: pulls bananas to you.
  • 🛡️ Jade Shield: survives one crash or fall.
  • ✖2 Double Score: doubles every point.
  • ⚡ Jungle Rush: you sprint, smash through everything and turn on your own.
Progress
  • Bananas are your money: upgrade power-ups and unlock new monkeys in the shop. Golden bananas count as 5.
  • Every mission you finish adds +1 to your score multiplier, permanently.
  • The jungle changes every 1,000 m: Emerald Jungle, Sunset Ruins, Moonlit Temple, Bamboo Mists.
